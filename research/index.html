Process

Engineering first, then writing: understand the system, learn your perspective, prepare it for your target audience.

  1. Understand the system

    I start with the system itself: how it works, what it is for and where it can fail.

  2. Know your perspective

    I learn what you need the document to do, who owns it and what you already know.

  3. Prepare it for your audience

    I write for the people who will use it, whether developers, reviewers or end users, at the level of detail they need.

  4. Check it

    I verify claims, examples and numbers against the system, and write acceptance criteria and edge cases where they matter.

Working style

I work independently. Give me the project and the audience, and I will plan, draft and check the document myself.
